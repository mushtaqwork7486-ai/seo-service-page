ABOUT THE SERVICE

Make every important page clearer for search engines and people

On-page SEO improves the content and page elements you control. The goal is to help search engines understand each page while making it genuinely useful for visitors who may become customers.

Page titles and meta descriptions

I review title tags and meta descriptions for clarity, relevance and useful differentiation. Each important page should communicate its topic and value accurately. Metadata can support stronger search snippets, but search engines may choose different snippets or rewrite titles.

Content, headings and search intent

A page should answer the questions behind the search, not repeat a keyword unnaturally. I review heading structure, topical coverage, readability, missing information and calls to action. Recommendations focus on helpful, original content that demonstrates the service or product clearly.

URLs, images and internal links

Clean descriptive URLs, meaningful image alt text and contextual internal links make a site easier to navigate. Internal links help visitors discover related information and help search engines understand relationships between pages. I identify opportunities without adding links that feel forced.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Will on-page SEO alone improve rankings?

It can address important relevance and usability issues, but results also depend on technical health, competition, authority and overall site quality.

Do you rewrite all website content?

Not necessarily. I first identify what is useful, what needs improvement and where new content would add value.
