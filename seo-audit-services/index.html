ABOUT THE SERVICE

Understand what is holding your website back

An SEO audit gives you a structured view of your website’s search performance and improvement opportunities. It should turn observations into clear priorities, not overwhelm you with a long list of tool warnings.

Technical SEO review

The audit checks crawlability, indexation signals, redirects, broken links, sitemap and robots directives, canonical tags, mobile usability and performance opportunities. Findings are explained with affected URLs and practical recommendations wherever possible.

On-page and content review

I review key page titles, headings, content relevance, internal linking, duplicate or thin pages and alignment with search intent. The goal is to identify pages that need better targeting, clearer information or stronger connections to related content.

Prioritized action plan

Findings are grouped by impact and implementation effort so you can decide what to fix first. The report can include issue descriptions, examples, recommended actions and opportunities for further research. Some fixes may require a developer or CMS administrator.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Will an audit automatically fix my website?

An audit identifies issues and recommends actions. Implementation is a separate step unless agreed as part of the project.

How often should I run an SEO audit?

A full review is useful periodically and after major website changes. Smaller checks can be done more frequently based on site activity.
