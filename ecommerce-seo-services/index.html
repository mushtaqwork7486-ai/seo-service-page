ABOUT THE SERVICE

Help shoppers find the products you sell

E-commerce SEO helps product and category pages appear for relevant searches throughout the shopping journey. It balances discoverability with a clear shopping experience and accurate product information.

Category and product page optimization

Category pages should explain the range of products available, while product pages should provide unique, useful details. I review titles, descriptions, headings, product attributes, image information and internal links to help each page serve a clear purpose.

Faceted navigation and technical structure

Filters and sorting can create many URL combinations. I assess crawl paths, indexation controls, canonical signals, pagination and duplicate content risks. The right setup depends on the platform and how shoppers use filters, so changes should be tested carefully.

Structured data and shopping content

Where appropriate, product structured data can help search engines interpret product details such as price, availability and ratings. Markup must match visible page content and stay current. Helpful buying guides and comparison content can also support shoppers researching a purchase.

Can SEO increase online sales?

SEO can attract relevant shoppers, but sales also depend on pricing, stock, shipping, trust, product quality and checkout experience.

Do you work with Shopify or WooCommerce?

The SEO principles apply to both platforms. Specific implementation depends on your store setup and available access.
