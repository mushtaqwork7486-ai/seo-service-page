ABOUT THE SERVICE

Earn relevant links that support long-term authority

Link building is most sustainable when it focuses on relevance, editorial value and real relationships. The objective is to earn useful references from appropriate websites—not to collect large numbers of low-quality links.

Backlink profile review

I review referring domains, link relevance, anchor text patterns and potential risks to understand your existing backlink profile. Metrics from third-party SEO tools are estimates, so they are considered alongside actual relevance and website quality.

Relevant outreach opportunities

Potential opportunities may include industry publications, partner resources, local organizations, useful directories and content that deserves to be referenced. Outreach should be personalized and transparent, with a clear reason the resource benefits the publisher’s audience.

Quality over quantity

I avoid private blog network schemes, automated spam, paid link schemes that violate search engine guidelines and irrelevant bulk submissions. No link campaign can promise a ranking increase. Progress is assessed through the quality and relevance of opportunities and the wider SEO performance.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How many backlinks will I get?

The number depends on your niche, content, outreach and publisher decisions. I do not promise a fixed number of links regardless of quality.

Do you use PBNs or automated links?

No. The approach is quality-focused and avoids PBNs, spam and manipulative bulk link tactics.
