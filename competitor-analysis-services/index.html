ABOUT THE SERVICE

Learn from the search landscape and find your next opportunity

SEO competitor analysis shows how other websites compete for the searches that matter to your business. It helps reveal content gaps and realistic opportunities while keeping your strategy focused on your own customers.

Identify true search competitors

Your business competitors are not always your SEO competitors. I identify websites appearing for your target searches and compare the pages and topics that earn visibility. This creates a more useful benchmark than comparing only company names.

Keyword and content gap analysis

I look for relevant queries competitors target that your website does not cover well, as well as topics where your pages may need clearer intent or better information. Opportunities are filtered for business relevance rather than copied blindly.

Backlink and page-level insights

Competitor pages and referring domains can reveal useful formats, resources and outreach prospects. Third-party metrics are directional estimates, not guarantees. The final output is a prioritized set of actions for your website, including pages to improve and content to consider.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Will you copy competitor content?

No. Competitor research is used to understand search expectations and identify gaps. Your content should be original and useful to your audience.

Which competitors should I provide?

You can share known business competitors, but I can also identify websites that compete with your target keywords in search results.
