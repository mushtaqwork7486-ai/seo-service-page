ABOUT THE SERVICE

Build a website search engines can crawl and understand

Technical SEO focuses on the foundations that allow search engines to access, render and index useful pages. It also considers the experience people have when they visit your website.

Crawling and indexation checks

I review robots.txt, XML sitemaps, indexability directives, canonical tags, redirects and important response errors. The aim is to help search engines discover the right URLs and avoid wasting attention on duplicate, broken or low-value pages.

Site structure and duplicate URL signals

A logical structure helps visitors and crawlers move through related topics. I look for orphan pages, confusing navigation, redirect chains, duplicate versions and inconsistent canonical signals. Recommendations are prioritized so important pages remain accessible and clearly connected.

Performance and mobile experience

I assess mobile usability and performance opportunities, including image weight, unnecessary scripts and Core Web Vitals signals where data is available. Not every metric issue has the same business impact, so recommendations explain likely value and practical next steps.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can technical SEO guarantee indexing?

No. Technical fixes can remove barriers and improve discovery, but search engines decide what to crawl and index.

Do you need access to my website?

An initial review can use public pages. Implementing fixes may require suitable CMS, hosting or developer access, depending on the issue.
