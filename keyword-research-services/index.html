ABOUT THE SERVICE

Reach the searches that matter to your business

Effective SEO starts with understanding what your audience searches for. Keyword research helps you discover relevant topics, assess competition and prioritize search terms that can attract qualified visitors—not just traffic.

What keyword research includes

A useful keyword plan looks beyond search volume. It considers intent, relevance, ranking difficulty, business value and the pages your website can realistically support. I review seed topics, keyword variations, long-tail searches, questions and location modifiers where relevant.

Search intent and keyword mapping

The same phrase can signal very different needs. Informational searches call for helpful guides, while commercial and transactional searches may need service or product pages. I group related terms by intent and map them to the most appropriate existing or planned URL to reduce overlap and keyword cannibalization.

A practical keyword strategy

You receive organized keyword clusters, primary and supporting terms, intent notes, suggested page targets and content opportunities. Priorities are based on relevance and realistic opportunities rather than promising specific rankings. The result can guide landing pages, blog content and ongoing optimization.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How many keywords do I need?

It depends on your website size and goals. A focused set of well-mapped keyword groups is usually more useful than a large unorganized list.

Do you guarantee ranking for selected keywords?

No. Rankings depend on competition, site quality, search changes and many other factors. Research helps make informed priorities, but no honest SEO provider can guarantee a position.
